Website Summary:

What content will the site contain?

The site will contain information about me, my mechanical engineering education, skills, projects, and experience. It will also include examples of my CAD work, engineering projects, and other work that shows what I have learned.

Where will you get all of the content for your site(ie. images & text)?

I will get most of the content from my own school projects and work experience. I will use pictures of my projects I have worked on, examples of my CAD designs, and information about my education and skills.

Why should users care about the focus of your site?

Users should care because the site will give them a better idea of my skills and interested in mechanical engineering. It will also show the type of work that I am able to do.

Why should a site be built for this?

A website is a good way to show off my work to potential employers, fellow engineers, and anyone that stumbles across my website. It puts everything that I have done all in one place, and allows me to show off my projects instead of just describing them.

What would you like users to do at your site?

I would like users to look through my projects and learn more about my skills as a mechanical engineer. I would also like potential employers to use the site to learn more about me and the work that I have done.

What is the goal of the site?

The goal of the site is to create a portfolio that shows my education, skills, projects, and experience in mechanical engineering. I want it to help me show what I have learned and what I can bring to future jobs.
