Competition:

What is my competition doing right?

Other mechanical engineering portfolios do a good job of showcasing what projects, skills and education they have. Many of them also use pictures and examples of their work to show what they have done.

What is my competition doing wrong?

Some engineering portfolios can be difficult to navigate or have too much stuff on one page. Some of them also don't do a good job of showing what they have done and just descibe it instead. The can make it harder for employers to understand what the person is able to do.

How can I do it better?

I can make my portfolio simple and easy to navigate while showing actual examples of my work. I will organize my projects, CAD work, education, and skills so that users can easily find the information. I also want the site to show my personality while still looking clean and professional.
